# RIGOR Partner / Investor Share Kit

## One-line positioning
RIGOR turns fragmented live-production information into one operational source of truth—showing what is required, what conflicts, who owns it, what remains unresolved, and whether the show is actually ready.

## 60-second spoken introduction
RIGOR is an operational intelligence platform for live production. Today, touring and venue teams advance shows across riders, venue packs, emails, confirmations, schedules, spreadsheets, and tribal knowledge. RIGOR pulls those inputs into one production truth, extracts the requirements, preserves the source evidence, identifies contradictions, assigns ownership, tracks readiness, and carries the same decisions into show day and the final Advance Report.

The guided sample is intentionally short. Confirm one requirement, inspect its evidence, resolve the production conflicts, and watch readiness change. The important part is that the report, department views, and show-day state all come from the same underlying production intelligence.

The long-term direction is an operational system of record for touring and venue production: a platform that becomes more useful as real production decisions, requirements, venues, and workflows move through it.

## What to test
1. Select **Partner / Investor** and open the guided sample production.
2. Review one requirement and open **Source evidence**.
3. Confirm or reject the remaining review decisions.
4. Move into **Technical advance**, resolve the conflicts, and verify ownership.
5. Move into **Show day**, complete checkpoints, and see how incidents affect readiness.
6. Export the **Master Advance Report** or a department report and confirm that it reflects the same production decisions.

## What to notice
- One source of truth instead of disconnected documents and conversations.
- Source-backed requirements rather than unsupported AI summaries.
- Readiness changes based on actual production decisions, conflicts, ownership, checkpoints, and incidents.
- Master and department reports are filtered views of the same underlying intelligence.
- AI/DeerFlow works underneath the workflow rather than becoming the workflow.
