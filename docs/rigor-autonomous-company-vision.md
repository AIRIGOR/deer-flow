# RIGOR Autonomous Company Vision

## Reference Model

The operating reference is the autonomous-company idea publicly demonstrated by
platforms such as Polsia: AI should not merely answer prompts; it should
proactively run company functions, preserve memory, detect work, execute
recurring tasks, and return only material decisions to the founder.

RIGOR adapts that model to a domain-specific company with a defensible
live-production product and explicit human authority boundaries.

## North Star

**Founder-led. AI-operated. RIGOR-powered.**

The Founder defines the mission, product thesis, ownership, and hard limits. The
AI company runs the recurring operating layer around RIGOR continuously enough
that progress does not depend on the Founder manually coordinating every
department.

## Company Structure

### Founder / Vision Authority
Owns mission, brand direction, ownership, equity, financing terms, contracts,
material spending, public commitments, production promotion, and irreversible
decisions.

### AI Chief of Staff
Maintains the company-level operating picture, selects priorities, reconciles
department conflicts, tracks durable state, and returns approval packets.

### Product + Production Intelligence
Owns operator workflows, production truth, requirements, readiness, Production
Handshake, change-impact intelligence, and product acceptance criteria.

### Engineering
Builds the platform, automation, APIs, internal tooling, tests, and safe
self-improvement loops.

### QA / Security / Reliability
Attempts to break RIGOR before users do; owns release gates, rollback evidence,
security, autonomous-action controls, and demo reliability.

### Market Intelligence
Tracks competitors, substitutes, customers, adjacent tools, white space, and
market-entry opportunities.

### Partnerships + Capital
Maintains accelerators, strategic partners, investors, grants, deadlines,
applications, diligence, and funding-readiness work. Prepares; does not bind.

### Growth + Revenue
Turns product proof into customer qualification, pilot offers, pricing
hypotheses, sales enablement, growth experiments, and measurable revenue paths.

### Customer Success
Owns onboarding, feedback triage, support intelligence, adoption, retention
signals, and the loop from operator evidence back into product.

### Finance + Runway
Models costs, unit economics, runway scenarios, capital needs, and financial
readiness without moving money.

## Autonomy Ladder

**Level 1 — Observe:** research, monitor, classify, remember.

**Level 2 — Prepare:** draft plans, code, applications, proposals, support
responses, campaigns, diligence, and decision packets.

**Level 3 — Internal Execute:** run tests, update internal state, implement code
inside allowed environments, prepare releases, update internal pipelines.

**Level 4 — Scoped External Execute:** future permissioned actions such as
approved outreach, support replies, campaigns, and integrations within explicit
Founder-approved policies, budgets, and rollback controls.

**Level 5 — Founder Reserved:** ownership, equity, financing acceptance, binding
contracts, material spending, production promotion, irreversible actions.

The target is not blind 100% autonomy. The target is maximum useful autonomy
inside explicit authority boundaries.

## Self-Improvement Loop

1. Agents observe product, customer, market, financial, and infrastructure state.
2. They create evidence-backed work.
3. Engineering implements approved internal changes.
4. QA attempts to break them.
5. Company memory records what changed.
6. The next cycle begins from prior state, not zero.
7. Repetitive Founder coordination is progressively converted into safe
   automation.

## Capital Vision

RIGOR's AI company should be capable of preparing its own funding path:
identifying opportunities, tracking deadlines, assembling evidence, generating
applications/pitch/diligence material, and detecting fundability gaps.

It cannot accept capital or financing terms without Founder approval.

## Commercial Vision

RIGOR should not depend on mass consumer volume. The company should build toward
high-value enterprise revenue from production organizations, venues, promoters,
vendors, touring teams, and related operators while preserving the data and
workflow moat created by real production usage.

## Durable Memory

Every operating cycle should preserve only material state: objectives,
milestones, relationships, feedback, risks, experiments, runway assumptions,
customer evidence, revenue evidence, capital opportunities, and proof artifacts.

## Success Condition

The Founder should increasingly be able to say:

**"Here is the vision and the boundary. Run the company, bring me the decisions
that actually require me, and show me proof of what happened."**
