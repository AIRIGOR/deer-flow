## Problem
Live production still runs critical operations across fragmented PDFs, venue technical packages, riders, spreadsheets, emails, crew notes, vendor communications, and individual memory. That fragmentation creates missed requirements, late answers, duplicated work, department conflicts, and operational knowledge that disappears after each show.

## Solution
RIGOR is an AI-native operating intelligence layer for live production. It ingests technical and hospitality documents, extracts and normalizes requirements, assigns department ownership, exposes conflicts, tracks readiness, records incidents and checkpoints, and generates a master Advance Report plus department-specific outputs with source provenance.

## Current stage
Working MVP / beta preview. The platform supports document ingestion, requirement extraction, department review, readiness, conflict resolution, incidents/checkpoints, Advance Report generation, and a persistent AI-company operating layer.

## Saudi market plan
RIGOR intends to enter Saudi Arabia through a controlled pilot with a venue, promoter, festival, or production company. The pilot would localize venue templates, terminology, and vendor categories; process 3–5 productions; and measure requirements resolved, time saved, conflict reduction, and readiness improvement before expanding into recurring workflows.
